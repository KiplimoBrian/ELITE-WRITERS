A simple and transparent process: apply once, get approved, access jobs, submit work, and withdraw your earnings.

1) Register & Apply

Create an account, complete your writer profile, and submit your application for review.

2) Get Approved

We verify your details and skills. Once approved, you unlock access to available writing jobs.

3) Work & Get Paid

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about getting started, jobs, and payments.

How do I get approved?

Complete your profile and submit your application. We review your details and notify you once approved.

How do payments work?

You withdraw earnings through the Payments page. Withdrawals are sent to your M-Pesa number.

Can I work anytime?

Yes. You can accept jobs when available and work on your schedule as long as you meet deadlines.

What happens if I miss a deadline?

Repeated missed deadlines can affect your access to jobs. Always communicate early if you need help.
