Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about writing with Elite Writers

How do I apply to become a writer?

Click the "Apply Now" button on any page, fill out the application form with your details, expertise, and a link to your writing samples. Our team reviews applications within 48 hours and will notify you via email.

What types of writing jobs are available?

We offer a wide range including academic essays, research papers, business proposals, blog posts, SEO content, technical writing, and editing/proofreading tasks.

How and when do I get paid?

Payments are made directly to your M-Pesa account. You can request a withdrawal anytime once your available balance reaches KES 500. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours.

What are the payment rates?

Rates vary by task complexity, deadline, and word count — typically ranging from KES 300 to KES 800 per page. Higher rates are available for specialized or urgent work.

Do I need specific qualifications?

While a degree is preferred, we prioritize strong writing skills. All applicants must pass a grammar test and submit quality writing samples during onboarding.

Can I choose my own assignments?

Yes! Available jobs are posted in your dashboard. You can browse and claim tasks that match your expertise and schedule.

Is there a minimum number of tasks I must complete?

No minimum requirement. You work at your own pace and choose how many assignments to take based on your availability.

How do revisions and quality control work?

Clients may request revisions. We encourage clear communication to ensure satisfaction. Repeated quality issues may affect your account standing.

Is Elite Writers available outside Kenya?

Yes! We welcome writers from anywhere, but payments are currently processed via M-Pesa (Kenya only). We are working on adding more international payment options soon.

Who can I contact for support?

Email us at support@elitewriters.co.ke or use the contact form on the site. We typically respond within 12 hours.
